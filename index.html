Professional profile

International preparation, U.S. classroom experience.

Dedicated elementary educator with international preparation and U.S. classroom experience. I hold a Bachelor's-equivalent in Elementary and Middle School Education with concentrations in Spanish, French and English, and a Master's-equivalent in Educational Psychology.

Teaching focus: Departmentalized elementary Mathematics and Science, bilingual content instruction, and inquiry-based learning. Proudly Colombian, bringing a multilingual perspective to U.S. classrooms.

I'm passionate about inclusive, student-centered instruction and bilingual learning. I hold a Colorado Interim Authorization — Teacher, Elementary Education (K–6), valid August 9, 2026 through August 9, 2027, and have demonstrated CEFR B2 English proficiency on the TOEFL iBT.

Teaching philosophy

Every child deserves an inclusive, engaging and academically challenging classroom where language, culture and curiosity are celebrated. My goal is to develop confident readers, critical thinkers and compassionate learners while partnering closely with families.
